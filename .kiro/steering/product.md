# Vybe Product

Vybe is a mobile-first social discovery and event coordination platform launching in Da Nang, Vietnam. It helps residents, travelers, expats, and digital nomads find in-person activities and feel comfortable attending alone. English, Vietnamese and Russian are planned languages.

Founder is an early event organizer, not the sole host. Platform should eventually support vetted third-party hosts. Beach Social #001 is **planned for 2026-12-05**, venue to be confirmed. An open fire is NOT promised until permissions are verified. Phase one prioritizes discovery, event details, RSVP, host operations, and a shareable web page. Telegram distribution is a later integration.

Product decisions and priorities are described in `docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md`.
