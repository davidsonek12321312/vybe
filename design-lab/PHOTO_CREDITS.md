# Stand-in photographs (sample events only)

These photographs illustrate the **fictional sample events** in the design-lab
prototype. They are stand-ins, not MOVA's own photography, and they are not
the places named in the sample events unless noted. All come from Wikimedia
Commons through the Taste skill's `photos.mjs search` (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or
public domain; 1600 px or wider), downloaded at 960 px (pottery at 500 px)
and bundled in `assets/images/sample/`. The event page shows each credit
under the story ("Photo: …").

Replace them with commissioned photography before any public launch. CC BY-SA
files require the credit and licence wherever they are shown.
